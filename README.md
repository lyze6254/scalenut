## The Feature That Everyone Talks About: Cruise Mode

If there's one thing Scalenut is known for, it's **Cruise Mode**. This is their flagship article writer, and it's genuinely impressive in how fast it moves.

Here's roughly how it works: you plug in a target keyword, Scalenut pulls data from the top-ranked articles on Google (SERP analysis), suggests a title, builds an outline, highlights NLP key terms your article needs to hit, and then generates the actual content. The whole process — from blank page to a draft — takes about 5 minutes.

Now, will you publish that draft as-is? Probably not. But here's where it gets interesting: the draft is structured around real competitor data, so it's not just random AI fluff. You get writing points, suggested statistics pulled from SERP research, FAQ sections sourced from Google, Reddit, and Quora, and even CTA options baked in. It's more like a really well-researched starting point than a finished product.

One G2 reviewer put it well — Cruise Mode helps you go "from idea to publish-ready in one sitting." That tracks with what most users report.
